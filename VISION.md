# Noodle Dragons — Vision Notes

Written in Phase 1 (Kickoff), 2026-11-07, from Jacob's answers to the five vibe questions.
Every later phase is checked against these notes.

## The one-line pitch

A cozy pet game about raising **super cute, glowing noodle dragons** in a nighttime den, with lo-fi music playing in the background.

## 1. Dragon feel: super cute

- Round, chubby noodle bodies with big sparkly eyes. They should feel like baby animals.
- **Cute Eastern dragons** (Jacob's redesign, 2026-11-09): long snaky bodies with fish scales, a spiny back ridge, swept-back antler horns, a flame-like mane, long whiskers from the nose, little clawed feet and a flame tail fin. A real tapered dragon muzzle (never a round pig snout) and big almond jewel eyes with brows.
- Soft, bouncy movement. Even the adults stay cute and never turn fierce.
- Expressions are big and easy to read: happy wiggles, sleepy droops, little pouts.

## 2. Colors: night and glow

- The world is deep blue, indigo and purple, like a starry night.
- Dragons, lanterns, food and decorations **glow** against the dark: soft neon pinks, cyans, golds and mint.
- Rare dragons get extra shimmer or sparkle trails.
- Night is the home look. Day mode (phase 22) is just a softer twilight, never harsh bright daylight.
- Colors must still be easy to tell apart for colorblind players (phase 47).

## 3. Music: lo-fi chill

- Relaxed lo-fi beats with soft keys and vinyl crackle.
- Sound effects are gentle: chirps, purrs, soft pops. Nothing loud or sharp.
- A mute switch is always easy to reach.

## 4. Must-haves

- **Lots of petting.** Petting is a star feature, not a side feature: tickle spots, glowing purrs, happy reactions (phase 16 deserves extra love).
- **Breeding and rare dragons.** Mixing genes, surprise mutations, and rare shimmery dragons worth chasing (phases 30, 38–40).
- **Dress-up and decorating.** Hats, bows and scarves on dragons, plus a den full of glowing lanterns, cushions and plants (phases 23, 33).

Mini-games were not picked as a must-have. The one planned mini-game (phase 26, Noodle Catch) stays small and optional.

## 5. Things to avoid

- **No stress and no timers.** No countdowns and no guilt for being away. Needs change slowly and gently, and a neglected dragon just gets sleepy (phase 20).
- **Nothing sad or scary.** Dragons never die, never cry for long, and nothing spooky happens. The night setting stays cozy, never creepy.
- **Very little reading.** Show things with icons, pictures and animation. Keep any text short, including the tutorial (phase 45).

## Still open

- Ads and real-money purchases were not ruled out in the vibe check. Jacob would like a premium currency (gems) after launch; see DECISIONS.md. The current plan has none (coins are earned only by playing, phase 24). Jacob decides later, together with the open question "free game, or a small one-time price?".
